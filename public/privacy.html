Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Cream & Crust ("the app", "we", "us") is a business-management application for home bakers and small bakeries. It helps you manage orders, customers, recipes, inventory, invoices, and a shareable public menu. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application and web services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we store your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and basic profile details shared by authentication providers (Google, Apple). Passwords are handled securely via Firebase Authentication and are never stored in readable form.

Business Profile: Bakery name, logo, tagline, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, website, city, pickup address, delivery areas, UPI ID, and GST number entered into your profile. These details appear on your invoices and public menu.

Customer & Order Data: Names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, and order notes entered for order fulfillment. Sensitive customer details are encrypted prior to storage.

Photos & Assets: Product photos, recipe images, logos, or payment QR codes uploaded to the app, hosted via secure cloud storage (Cloudinary / Firebase).

Subscriptions & Purchases: Plan entitlement, status, and purchase timestamps managed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We never collect or store your payment cards, bank credentials, or UPI PINs.

2. Device Permissions

  • Camera: To take photos for products, recipes, or customer order references.
  • Photos & Media: To upload existing gallery images for your bakery menu and invoices.
  • File Storage: To export and save PDF invoices, shopping lists, and business reports.
  • Notifications: To deliver real-time order alerts and inventory notifications.

3. AI Features (Cream AI)

Cream & Crust includes optional AI assistance (Cream AI). When triggered, relevant bakery data snapshots (orders, inventory, products) are processed via secure API gateways (OpenRouter). AI providers do not retain data for model training.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database (Firestore), push messaging, performance & Crashlytics.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Subscription management.
  • Cloudinary: Image media hosting.

5. Data Rights & Deletion

You can view, edit, or permanently delete your account and associated data directly within the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. You may also contact us to exercise your data principal rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable international privacy laws.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@creamandcrust.online.
